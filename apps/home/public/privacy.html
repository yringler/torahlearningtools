Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the apps published by Yehuda Ringler under the name Torah Learning Tools:

  • Mishnah Ba'al Peh (By Heart, משנה בעל פה): the website byheart.torahlearningtools.com and its Android app.
  • Word Quiz (Cheder Flashcards): the website chederflashcards.torahlearningtools.com and its Android app.
  • This website, torahlearningtools.com.

The Android apps show the same websites inside the app. They collect nothing beyond what the websites collect, and they ask for no Android permissions except internet access.

In short: we collect as little as we can and never sell it. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and nothing is shared for advertising.

Word Quiz (Cheder Flashcards)

Word Quiz has no accounts and collects no personal information. Your child's progress (which word lists are learned and test scores) is saved only on the device, in the browser's or app's local storage. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes it.

Mishnah Ba'al Peh (By Heart)

Without an account (guest mode): progress is saved only on the device and is never sent to us.

With an account, which a parent creates so progress syncs between devices, we store:

  • The parent's email address, to send sign-in emails (a link and a 6-digit code) and to identify the account. We don't send marketing emails.
  • Children's profiles that the parent creates: the name or nickname the parent types in, a color, an emoji and app settings.
  • Learning progress for each child: which mishnayos were practiced, when, and how it went (for example grades, how many hints were used, and how long practice took). The app uses this to schedule review and to show the parent how the child is doing.
  • Sign-in records: a session record with the device's browser description (user agent), kept until 30 days after the session was last used. During sign-in we briefly keep a one-way hash of the IP address to prevent abuse. It is deleted within about a day.

The sign-in page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that the person signing in isn't a bot. Turnstile looks at technical information about the browser and connection. It does not use cookies for advertising.

The app never records audio or video. Children recite out loud to a parent, and the parent grades the recitation by tapping buttons.

Who processes the data

  • Cloudflare hosts all the websites and stores account data (Cloudflare Workers and D1). Like any web host, Cloudflare processes IP addresses and request details to deliver the pages and protect against attacks. It keeps short-lived request logs. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Resend sends Mishnah Ba'al Peh's sign-in emails, so it receives the email address and the email's contents. Resend's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on the torahlearningtools.com home page, so Google receives the visitor's IP address when the page loads. The two apps don't use Google Fonts.
  • Google Play delivers the Android apps. Google's own policies cover what Google Play collects.

We don't sell, rent or share personal information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Children

Both apps are made for children to learn with a parent's help. Word Quiz collects no personal information at all. In Mishnah Ba'al Peh, only a parent can create an account (with the parent's own email address). The parent decides what goes in each child's profile and can edit or delete it at any time. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

All connections use HTTPS. Sign-in links and codes expire after 15 minutes and work once. We store only hashes of session tokens and sign-in codes, never the tokens themselves. Access to the data is limited to the developer.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Account data is kept until you delete the account. You can delete a child's profile or the whole account at any time. See how to delete your account. Deleting the account removes the email address, every child's profile and all progress from our database right away. Copies in Cloudflare's automatic database backups expire within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data by emailing us. Most of it you can already view, change or delete in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters for account holders, such as collecting a new kind of data, we'll email them first.
