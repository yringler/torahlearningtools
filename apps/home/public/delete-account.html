Delete your account

Mishnah Ba'al Peh (By Heart, משנה בעל פה), by Yehuda Ringler

Only Mishnah Ba'al Peh has accounts. Word Quiz (Cheder Flashcards) has no accounts and keeps progress only on the device. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear the site's data in the browser.

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open Mishnah Ba'al Peh (byheart.torahlearningtools.com or the Android app) and sign in.
  2. On the screen where you pick a child, press and hold 🔒 Parent (hold) to open the Parent area.
  3. In the Account section, open Delete account, type your account's email address to confirm, and tap Delete account.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, email admin@torahlearningtools.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and the account itself
  • Every child's profile (name, color, emoji, settings)
  • All learning progress and practice history
  • All sign-in sessions and pending sign-in requests

These are removed from our database right away. Copies in Cloudflare's automatic database backups expire within 30 days. We keep nothing else afterwards.

To delete one child instead of the whole account, open that child's card in the Parent area and tap Delete.

See also our privacy policy.
