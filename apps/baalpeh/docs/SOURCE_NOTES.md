# Where the sources differ (Steinsaltz vs. Bartenura)

This is a reference only; no action is needed. The Simple lines follow Steinsaltz, and Bartenura is used only where
Steinsaltz leaves a word untranslated. Both are legitimate readings. These notes were collected while each piece was
tied to its sources, in case you're curious.

## Perek 3–4
- **3:2:** Steinsaltz says start Shema only if you can finish the whole thing before reaching the mourners' rows. Bartenura says finishing one section is enough. The Simple line follows Steinsaltz.
- **3:4–3:6:** Bartenura notes that Ezra's rule requiring a mikvah before Torah and davening was later cancelled. Steinsaltz doesn't mention this. The Simple lines keep these topics vague on purpose.
- **4:1:** Steinsaltz says Maariv has no fixed time and can be said all night. Bartenura reads the wording as showing Maariv was once optional (today it is required).
- **4:4:** For פָּרָשַׁת הָעִבּוּר, Steinsaltz leaves the meaning to the Gemara, and the Simple line says "at every time of change". Bartenura says "even when they stray into sin" (shown as an explanation). The Simple line's "makes his davening a chore" follows Bartenura; Steinsaltz just says "fixed".
- **4:5:** Steinsaltz says the rider gets off his donkey to daven. Bartenura says the halacha doesn't follow this, and he does not get off.

## Perek 7–8
- **7:1:** Bartenura explains נָכְרִי as a convert who had milah but no tevilah. He also notes that today no zimmun is made with a Kuti.
- **7:3:** Bartenura explains that the first part follows R. Akiva and the rest follows R. Yosi HaGelili. He rules that only the wording for 3 and for 10 is used, and that the halacha follows R. Yishmael.
- **7:5:** For undiluted wine, Steinsaltz says no bracha is made. Bartenura says the bracha is borei pri ha'etz, and that the halacha does not follow R. Eliezer.
- **8:2:** Steinsaltz has "mix water into the wine", and Bartenura has "pour the cup". The Simple line follows Steinsaltz.
- **8:6:** Steinsaltz has "spices of gentiles". Bartenura explains these as spices from idol worshippers' parties, and the candle as one a non-Jew used on Shabbos.
- **8:7:** Bartenura says "digested" means until he feels hungry again. He also says that someone who left without bentching on purpose must go back, according to everyone.

## Perek 9
- **9:2:** Steinsaltz leaves *zikin* and *zeva'os* untranslated. "Shooting stars" and "earthquakes" come from Bartenura.
- **9:2, "the Great Sea":** Bartenura says it is the ocean that surrounds the world. An earlier draft said "the Mediterranean", which is in neither source, so it was removed.
- **9:5, "thank Him very, very much":** the "very, very much" is only in Bartenura. Steinsaltz just says "thank Him".
- **9:5, minim:** Steinsaltz says these are Sadducees, who denied Olam Haba. Bartenura says they are heretics who denied techiyas hameisim.
- **9:5, "min ha'olam":** Steinsaltz translates it as "until everlasting". Bartenura reads it as "from the world" (this world only), which is why the Sages added "until the world".

## Perek 1–2
- **1:4:** Steinsaltz reads אַחַת אֲרֻכָּה וְאַחַת קְצָרָה as "whether long or short", and the Simple line follows him. Bartenura reads it as the two brachos before Shema.
- **1:5:** Steinsaltz says R. Elazar ben Azarya was "about seventy", and the Simple line follows him. Bartenura says he was young but looked seventy.
- **2:1:** The words וְאִם לָאו לֹא יָצָא aren't in Steinsaltz's text, so that piece follows Bartenura.
- **2:6, אִסְטְנִיס:** Steinsaltz has "delicate"; Bartenura has "catches cold easily".

## Perek 5–6 (Simple lines follow Steinsaltz)
- **5:5, מְטֹרָף:** Steinsaltz has "my prayer is rejected". Bartenura's first explanation is that the sick person is "torn away".
- **6:3, נוֹבְלוֹת:** Steinsaltz has dates that spoiled. Bartenura has fruit that fell before it was ripe.
- **6:5:** Bartenura says the wine before the meal covers the wine after it only on Shabbos and Yom Tov.
- **6:6, מֻגְמָר:** Steinsaltz says the bracha is made by whoever said the bracha on the wine. Bartenura says it is whoever led bentching.
