משנה בעל פה (By Heart Mishna) helps your child learn mishnayos by heart.

• Each mishnah is learned in small pieces, with hints that fade from the full text to first letters to nothing.
• Then your child says it to you, and you grade it.
• Learned mishnayos come back for review on a spaced schedule, until a whole perek can be said in one go.
• Hebrew text with nikud. Tap any word to see what it means, with simple English and Steinsaltz explanations.

Use it as a guest, or make a free parent account to keep several children's progress in sync across devices. No ads.
